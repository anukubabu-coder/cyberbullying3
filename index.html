Anti-Bullying Guidelines for Bhutanese Schools

In accordance with Ministry guidelines and the Child Care and Protection Act of Bhutan, all educational institutions including Bjishong CS (Gasa) maintain zero-tolerance policies toward bullying and online cyber-harassment.

School & Hostel Bullying

Physical or verbal intimidation taking place on school grounds, dormitories, dining halls, or sports fields.

  • Physical: Pushing, tripping, hitting, or damaging uniform/textbooks.
  • Verbal: Insulting nicknames, mockings, or extortion of pocket money.
  • Hostel: Intimidation of junior students in boarding houses.

Cyberbullying Online

Digital harassment on WeChat groups, Facebook pages, Instagram, TikTok, or online gaming channels.

  • Group Chat Abuse: Sharing humiliating photos or fake rumors in class chats.
  • Fake Profiles: Creating fake accounts to impersonate or bully classmates.
  • Online Threats: Sending threatening messages or blackmailing.

Sherig Digital Safety

Cyberbullying Precautions by App

Essential guidelines and safety measures for popular social media apps in Bhutan.

Golden Rules for Cyber Safety

1. Preserve Evidence Take clear screenshots showing date, time, username, and message before deleting any chats.
2. Do Not Retaliate Do not reply with abusive words. Block the user immediately to prevent further harm.
3. Report Directly Submit the report through this portal directly to your School Principal or Counselor.

Platform-Specific Safety Instructions

WeChat (Class & Hostel Groups)
High Student Usage
  • Group Admin Reporting: Request class teacher/group admin to remove bullies from group chats.
  • Privacy Settings: Go to Me > Settings > Privacy and disable "Add Me by Group".
  • Evidence Collection: Long-press abusive text messages or voice notes, tap "Save" or screenshot chat logs.
Facebook & Messenger
Fake Profiles & Rumors
  • Fake Account Reporting: Click three dots `...` on offender profile > Select "Find Support or Report Profile".
  • Lock Profile: Enable "Profile Lock" so strangers cannot download or screenshot your photos.
  • Restrict Messages: Use Messenger's "Restrict" feature to silence messages without notifying offender.
TikTok (Video Harassment)
Bullying & Mockery Videos
  • Report Video: Tap the Share Arrow > Report > Harassment or Bullying.
  • Comment Filters: In Settings > Privacy > Comments, enable "Filter All Comments".
  • Private Account: Set TikTok account to Private so only approved classmates view videos.
